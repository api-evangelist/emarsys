The article examines why loyalty programs struggle, applying BJ Fogg's behavioral model (motivation, ability, trigger) to identify common failures. Most programs rely too heavily on points rather than creating emotionally engaging experiences, making participation feel difficult, and failing to deliver timely reminders that prompt customer action.
